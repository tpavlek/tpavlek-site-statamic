{{# Visible breadcrumb trail. Expects `breadcrumbs` — the list of rungs from App\Schema\BreadcrumbSchema, the same array the BreadcrumbList JSON-LD is built from, so the trail a reader sees and the one Google reads can't drift apart. Design notes, since a breadcrumb is easy to leave looking like leftover markup: - 13px rather than `text-sm`. This is signage, not content; at 14px it competed with the lede beneath it, and at 12px it stopped being comfortably tappable. - The chevron is an inline SVG, not a "/" or a "›". Punctuation separators inherit the text's weight and colour and land on the baseline, so they read as part of the words either side; a stroked chevron sits on the optical centre and can be muted well below the link colour without becoming invisible. - Separators are drawn by the rung that follows them, hidden on the first, which is what keeps the gaps even. Nesting them inside each item with its own gap gave two different spacings either side of every chevron — the "haphazard" part. - Linked rungs never wrap: "Edmonton Fringe Reviews (2026)" breaking across two lines reads as two crumbs. The last rung is a show title of unpredictable length, so it's the one thing allowed to wrap, and it wraps as a block rather than mid-phrase. - `label`, not `name` — the reader-facing form. See BreadcrumbSchema::crumb. - `path`, not `url` — relative, so a crumb on a preview host stays on that host. #}} {{ if breadcrumbs }}
