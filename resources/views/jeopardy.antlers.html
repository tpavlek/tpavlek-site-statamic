I lied about Starcraft Jeopardy returning

Oh boy, wouldn't it be great if this video was true? Unfortunately, the passion is just dead. It was made in a brief period where I was watching and enjoying StarCraft again. I just couldn't keep it up, and would do the project a huge disservice by writing questions without having expertise. For now, enjoy the immortalised episodes of the people you know and love from the StarCraft community competing in the singular wordplay-focused question-and-answer style of Jeopardy!

The dream is as dead as ded gaem.
