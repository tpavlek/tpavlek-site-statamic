With another round of revisions, this could be a five-star show. The performances are great, the music is novel and fun and when the show decides to hit, it hits **hard**. It's one where you might legitimately both laugh and cry, in the span of a single minute.

It employs some nonlinear storytelling and works ahead of the audience a bit *too much* in the first fifteen minutes. But like a good Chris Nolan movie (Memento, The Prestige), it will be worth it by the end. You're not getting Tenet here.
