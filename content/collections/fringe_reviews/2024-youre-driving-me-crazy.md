The big fringe shows with big productions and huge, inventive sets can be fun. This is not that. This is two girls, on stage in a car, driving to a wedding. And then they talk, and bicker. That's it, but it's executed flawlessly.

They talk a million miles a minute and in many ways it felt like watching an episode of Gilmore Girls crossed with Seinfeld.

Both performers absolutely nail every line and hit each joke with impeccable comedic timing. A surprise standout of the Fringe.
