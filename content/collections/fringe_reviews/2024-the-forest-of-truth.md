Sometimes shows cannot be explained, only experienced. This is an example of that. The show is *incredibly* Japanese, and sometimes it feels like watching real life anime.

If that anime had cartoonish hyper-sexualization.

Don't bring your family to this one (kids, nor your parents). You'll be left afterwards wondering *what* you saw, but you certainly will have been enthralled the entire time seeing it.
