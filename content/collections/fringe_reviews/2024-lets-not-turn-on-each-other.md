I cannot overemphasize how exceptionally stupid this show is - but intentionally.

I was absolutely mesmerized by the performance. I kept expecting it to go... SOMEWHERE? and it never did. There were bits that clearly took a tonne of effort, costuming and design to prep… for a five second joke. Never came back. Wild.

Fifteen question marks out of five stars.
