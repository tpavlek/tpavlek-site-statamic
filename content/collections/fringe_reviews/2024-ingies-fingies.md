Anyone who's seen Ingrid's past work, Epidermis Circus, will know that this is a must-watch. This show takes some of the best bits from Epidermis Circus, tightens them up, and adds a compelling overarching story.

Who knew I could care so much about a blob of clay or hate gummy bears with such extreme passion.

As a bonus, this show is also **FAMILY FRIENDLY** - if you're looking for a non-patronizing show that the whole family can enjoy this is the one. But don't feel the need to bring children. I didn't, and it was still five stars.
