The cost of tearing out Edmonton's bike lanes could be up to $170M.

Rumours have been swirling that the UCP is looking for costs for undoing all on-street bike infrastructure in major cities.

An internal City of Edmonton email has costed the removal of these lanes at $150-170M which, seems like a lot of money to waste on nothing, but we just spent $130M on a glorified opinion poll.

The legislature resumes this week and expect the UCP to continue their record of punishing cities and forcing them to spend more, and get nothing. We'll see just how far they go.
