Edmonton’s city budget is increasingly becoming just the province’s budget. The UCP has prohibited council from making some cuts, and forced their votes on increasing spending.

The largest line item in the budget, the police, will cost $567M next year, will have $56M of guaranteed increases in the next four years, and the province has indicated that if Edmonton reduces that, they will step in and force the budget to increase.Meanwhile the UCP has removed the ability for city council to approve the budgets for seniors housing facilities, while simultaneously mandating that Edmonton must pay the deficits in those budgets.

Council will be forced by the UCP to vote in favour of spending money we don’t have on sprawl early next year.

Your tax increase this year will be courtesy of Mayor Danielle Smith.
