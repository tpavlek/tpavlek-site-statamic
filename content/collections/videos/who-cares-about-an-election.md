"Who cares about an election? No one does."

That's what organizers behind the campaign opposing the zoning bylaw have to say about local democracy. And they walk the talk: they used their connections to the Premier and she took the extraordinary, unprecedented step of overturning zoning bylaws in cities in the province, and dictating how councillors and mayors can vote in the future. "Who cares".

The organizers of these anti-infill campaigns are not in the majority, and they don't represent the views of most Edmontonians, who show up at the polls to vote.

So why are we letting this vocal minority with UCP connections dictate policy province-wide?
