When she was announcing her shortsighted and anti-democratic overturning of Edmonton's zoning bylaw, she said she would force Edmonton to have a public hearing, and then dictate their votes.

That's against the law.

Councillors must enter public hearing with an open mind and listen to the perspectives in the hearing. But if they do, they will violate the province's directive.

Once again, the UCP has announced something they haven't even begun to think through.
