Should Edmonton just build its own hospitals?

We used to! Hospitals used to be municipally controlled by local hospital boards. But as healthcare grew, and there were more and more boards, eventually everything was consolidated under provincial control through AHS (now exploded, thanks, Danielle).

Under current legislation, municipalities can't operate hospitals and if they build something, they need the minister's approval to even put the word "hospital" on the sign.

So, no, we can't anymore. Edmonton doesn't get a vote on whether it gets a new hospital, and the people in control have decided to cancel our's.
