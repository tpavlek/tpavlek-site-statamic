Is Harman Kandola gearing up to leave Better Edmonton?

Kandola, who's running in Sspomitapi, has never been a strong supporter of Tim Cartmell, and was one of the two candidates openly questioning Tim's leadership on infill.

Now, he's appearing closer than ever to mayoral candidate Michael Walters, and seems to have divorced his branding entirely from Better Edmonton.

He has until nomination day on September 22nd to make his decision, after which the ballot will be final.
