If you're paying for parking, the city thinks you're a sucker.

Despite an explosion in population and more parking zones with more hours, the number of parking fines the city has issued has plummeted in the past decade.

Parking revenue is down, enforcement and fines are down, and compliance is down.

Now the city wants to add disruptive new parking rules, but they're still not enforcing the ones already on the books.
