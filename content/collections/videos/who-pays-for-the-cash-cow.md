You may not get a ticket in the mail anymore, but you're still paying.

Since the UCP removed photo radar in Alberta, collisions have increased, including fatal collisions. Insurance has gone through the roof. 

And now, taxes have to go up, too.

Before, risky drivers helped bear the cost of the consequences for what they did. Now, we all pay for it.

But hey, at least we killed that cash cow.
