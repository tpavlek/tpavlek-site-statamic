Privatization is a scam.

We now know just how bad a deal the privatization scheme Stephen Harper forced on Edmonton was.

The private contractor running the Valley Line is so expensive, in two years of running it ourselves we'll have saved enough money to pay the ENTIRE 30-year termination fee.

When you force public services to generate private profit, everything gets more expensive and worse.
