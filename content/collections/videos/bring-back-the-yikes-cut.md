Friends, I'm writing today with a plea for justice. In one of the land acknowledgements, Murray Utas said "I've been fringing for over 30 years... yikes".

But this year, the #yegfringe has cut the "yikes".

Theatre is about bringing people together. And I have never felt closer to my fellow Edmontonians, than sitting in a dark theatre, hearing Murray say he’d been fringing for 30 years, and then in unison, half the theatre going: “... Yikes”.

I need your help. Share this video, write your MLA, and email murray.utas@fringetheatre.ca and DEMAND the return of the YIKES cut.

Together, we can make a difference ❤️
