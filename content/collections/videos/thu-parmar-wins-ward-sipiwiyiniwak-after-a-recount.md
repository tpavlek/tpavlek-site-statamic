        I was close. The next day, Edmonton Elections [explained](https://globalnews.ca/news/11492129/edmonton-election-sipiwiyiniwak-vote-discrepency/) it was a transposition error. Counters at the Expo Centre found Roger Kotch missing from an advance-vote tally sheet and wrote him in by hand.         There was no citywide recount. Tim Cartmell [called for an audit](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/citywide-audit-needed-for-municipal-election-process-says-mayoral-runner-up-9.6950766), and in January the [city auditor's report](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-election-city-auditor-report-9.7062131) found long lineups, inadequate training and ballot boxes that weren't sealed properly.
