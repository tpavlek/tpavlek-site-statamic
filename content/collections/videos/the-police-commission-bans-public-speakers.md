Police Commission meetings will be held online-only with no public speakers for the rest of the year.

The Edmonton Police costs over half a billion dollars a year, the single most expensive service in the City of Edmonton. But the board tasked with keeping them accountable is refusing to hear from the public.

All to carry water for the Police Chief's ill-advised trip to Israel, to learn policing from the perpetrators of a genocide.

Removing the public is not accountability.
