          On October 19, four years after the charge, Duncan Kinney [pleaded guilty](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/kinney-pleads-guilty-mischief-9.7398539) to mischief. He admitted he spray-painted the face of the Shukhevych bust and wrote "Actual Nazi" on its base shortly after midnight on August 10, 2021. Then he wrote it up for the Progress Report that same day as the work of "an unknown person or persons." He'll be sentenced January 25, 2027.

          I wrote that he may be guilty, he may be innocent. He was guilty. The rest of this timeline still happened, and I've left it as I wrote it in 2022.

          - id: f66b0fb5

              **Ashvin Singh**, police commissioner, with an intention to leave his position to move to Vancouver, [writes a letter to Mayor Sohi](https://edmontonjournal.com/news/local-news/edmonton-police-commissioner-asks-mayor-to-investigate-councillor-over-ethical-issue) asking him to "investigate" **Stevenson** for "ethical issues".

              In the letter he says **Anne Stevenson** has tried to "actively influence an investigation" of **Duncan Kinney**.

              It is not clear why a police commissioner would know about an investigation of an individual, and if he did, telling others, like the mayor, about the investigation would seem to violate confidence. Singh has never responded to either of these questions.

              Despite requests from **Kinney's lawyer**, there has never been an answer as to whether **Kinney** was actually under investigation at this time, nor what the purported investigation was for.

              It's not clear who leaked this letter to the media.
